# Quorum overview

This file gathers what is **public** (website) and what people said about the company's history and its position in the group. What is internal and technical comes from the meetings and is in the other files. Where sources disagree, the disagreement is in "Gaps and open questions".

## Who it is

- Name: **Quorum Information Technologies Inc.** (QuorumInfoTech).
- Provides **software and services for car dealerships**. According to the website, it is a North American SaaS and services company with "deep roots in the automotive industry" that treats its dealership customers (in North America) as partners. The stated mission is to deliver integrated solutions that simplify dealership operations, including digital retailing tools for the consumer.
- Publicly traded company, listed on the **TSX Venture Exchange** under the symbol **QIS**, according to the website.
- According to Rick's diagram, Quorum is part of the **Valsoft** group (Valsoft appears above Quorum). The website does not mention Valsoft.

## History (Sam's informal account)

Sam said, informally, that Quorum was founded about **30 years** ago. The founder created the software for his parents' dealership, starting with a few people in a garage. This is a story told in conversation, **not confirmed** by an official source.

## Solution areas (website organization)

The website groups the offering into five areas: **DMS** (service and parts, sales, accounting, communications and analytics), **CRM** (service CRM, AI Marketing and sales CRM), **BDC** (outsourced relationship center), **Sales Retailing** (desking, F&I menu, digital retailing, accessories and marketplace) and **Service Retailing** (PowerLane, online scheduling, declined services and Tire Boost). The detail of each one is in [products/brands.md](../products/brands.md).

The **Analytics** item in the DMS area is **Q Analytics**: the DMS page cites it by name.
